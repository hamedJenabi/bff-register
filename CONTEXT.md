# Blues Fever Registration

The language used for festival admission and participants' class, competition, and lunch choices.

## Language

**Participant**:
A person registered to attend the festival with their own festival pass.
_Avoid_: Customer, account

**Festival registration**:
A participant's admission record, including their pass and any class, competition, or lunch choices.

**Full Pass**:
A festival pass that includes access to classes and parties.

**Party Pass**:
A festival pass that includes parties but excludes class registration.

**Parent Pass**:
A festival pass for a parent attending in coordination with another parent; each parent has their own festival registration.

**Class session**:
One scheduled occurrence of a class, with teachers, a room, and a time slot. Repeated occurrences can share a title without being the same session.
_Avoid_: Track, when referring to a single scheduled occurrence

**Time slot**:
A day and time interval during which several class sessions can run in parallel. A participant can select at most one class session in that interval.

**Daily class allowance**:
The maximum number of class sessions a participant can book on one festival day: five, further limited by one session per time slot. Each session is selected independently, including repeated or numbered track sessions.

**Registration draft**:
A participant's unsubmitted class, competition, and lunch choices. Adding a class to a draft does not reserve a place.

**Class booking**:
A saved selection of a class session and, for a partner class, a dance role. A booking may be provisional while payment is pending or confirmed after a successful class-only submission or payment.

**Provisional booking**:
A submitted class booking awaiting payment. It occupies a place for up to one hour while checkout remains open and is released if checkout expires without payment.

**Confirmed booking**:
A class booking accepted after a successful class-only submission or confirmed payment. An unsuccessful edit leaves the previous confirmed booking in place.

**Partner class**:
A class session for which participants select either lead or follow when registering.

**Dance role**:
Lead or follow in a partner class or an applicable competition.

**Competition entry**:
A participant's registration to compete in a contest, distinct from attending a class about competition.

**Lunch booking**:
A participant's choice to have a meal at the venue on a particular festival day.

**Paid add-on**:
A lunch booking or competition entry purchased in addition to the festival pass. Participants can add one through a later checkout; organizers handle removal and refunds.
